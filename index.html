What we heard

Start with the work, then choose the technology

The joint direction is to make the seller experience simpler, protect Dynamics 365 as the system of record, and prove value before expanding the solution.

Reduce seller administration

Capture signals once, remove unnecessary steps, and make safe follow-through easier from Outlook, Teams, mobile, and the flow of work.

Use the right layer

Begin with ready-now Copilot and Sales Agent value, use Cowork for justified cross-app orchestration, and extend only when a real gap remains.

Keep people in control

Ground experiences in approved data, keep proposed actions reviewable, and require confirmation for consequential updates.

From vision to working proof

Move forward in bounded, measurable steps

The path separates what can be enabled now from what must be discovered, proven, and governed before scale.

1 · Align

Map roles and work

Choose priority seller personas, recurring tasks, friction, approved context, and outcome measures.

2 · Activate

Use ready-now value

Validate the Sales Agent, Copilot, environment, access, and support path for a bounded cohort.

3 · Prove

Test one orchestration

Run one cross-app scenario with explicit data, actions, human approvals, cost routing, and stop measures.

4 · Decide

Scale, adjust, or stop

Expand only when repeat use, usefulness, data quality, supportability, and cost evidence justify it.

Technical delivery set

Discovery-first technical direction

The current state is a customer journey and discovery guide, not a final production design. Haworth and Microsoft technical owners will close the decisions below before implementation.

Technical details

Readiness to confirm

  • Target environment and eligible users
  • Identity, roles, data, and licensing path
  • Support, monitoring, and rollback ownership
Architecture

Boundary to validate

  • Dynamics 365 remains the system of record
  • Copilot, Sales Agent, and Cowork use approved context
  • Connectors and actions are explicitly bounded
Solution design

Proof to define

  • One persona, task, trigger, and desired outcome
  • Human review for consequential actions
  • Acceptance, cost, stop, and scale measures

Decision owner: joint Haworth and Microsoft technical leads, informed by the seller-process discovery and the selected proof scenario.
